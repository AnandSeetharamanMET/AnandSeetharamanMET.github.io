01About

I'm a Mechanical Engineering Technology graduate (B.S., Kennesaw State University) currently working as an Integration Technician on SpaceX's Starlink production line in Redmond, WA. Day to day I assemble, test, and verify flight hardware, troubleshoot parts that don't conform, and pass what I see on the build floor back to engineering so recurring issues get fixed at the root instead of reworked again and again.

Before aerospace, I spent about three years as an automotive technician and another few solving operational problems at The Home Depot. That's where I learned structured troubleshooting: isolate the fault, verify the fix, document it. In school I added CAD and simulation, earning my CSWP and a Dassault Systèmes mechanical design certificate in CATIA / 3DEXPERIENCE. For senior design I was the lead CAD designer and FEA analyst on a five-person team: I designed the full mechanism of a forklift-mounted barrel gripper in SolidWorks and ran all of its simulation work.

I'm now looking to move into a manufacturing, test, or mechanical engineering role in the Tacoma–Seattle area, where I can combine hands-on production experience with design and analysis tools to improve how hardware gets built.
